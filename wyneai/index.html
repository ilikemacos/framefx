WyneAI

The assistant built into Universal-FrameFX. Ask it anything, have it set up your games, and let it run FrameFX for you. It runs on your own PC, so your chats stay with you.

What it does

Chat about anything. Questions, ideas, quick help. It can look things up on the web and open a page to read it for you.

Set up your games. Say "set up Cyberpunk for my laptop, make it quieter" and it builds a preset for that game, then lets you apply it.

Run FrameFX. Start or stop FrameFX on a game, change settings, or ask what's running right now.

Open your apps. Ask it to open Steam or another app you've allowed.

Plays fair. It won't turn on frame generation for games that use anti-cheat.

You stay in charge

WyneAI asks before it does anything. Applying a preset, starting FrameFX or opening an app always shows a Confirm box first, with what will change. Press Cancel and nothing happens.

Setup

1. Install Universal-FrameFX for Windows 10 or 11.

2. Click WyneAI (experimental) in the app. The first time, run agent\scripts\Setup-FrameFXAgent.ps1 from the FrameFX folder. It's a one-time download of about 2 GB.

3. Chat in the app, or right here on this page while WyneAI is open in FrameFX.

Chat

Not connected
